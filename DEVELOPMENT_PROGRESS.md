## Phase 2 — Core football gameplay repair (2026-10-13)
**Implemented (`src/game.js`):**
- Passing: launch solved for an arrival speed `min(15, 8.5 + d·0.14)` × power `(0.7 + 0.6·pw)` × technique; driven passes carry topspin; rolling resistance raised (`BALLK.mu` 1.0→1.6, `grass` .28→.33) so missed passes stop sooner; `humanPass` cross crash fixed, direct fallbacks at 12–27 m/s.
- Receiving: every pass target (through balls too) gets the 1.8 s meet-the-ball run; the stick only overrides it when pointed >60° away from the ball.
- Dribbling as real touches: the ball rolls on its own physics between touches; a touch sends it ahead in the stick/AI direction off alternating feet by `push = (0.35 + 0.15·speed)·(1.25 − drib/160)` metres (speed from `sqrt(2·decel·push)`), shield/standing touches are tiny; a sharp change of direction kills the ball first (next touch sends it the new way); the carrier steers to collect the ball; it can get away (>1.9 m + speed·0.1) and an opponent on its path can poke it off (rate 1.6 + (def−drib)/25 per second). Owner turn penalty removed; lateral grip 11 + drib/18; facing rate `max(7, 16 − speed)`.
- First touch cushion: incoming velocity scaled by `0.3 − drib/400` plus the player's momentum instead of dying instantly; chest/thigh traps keep the ball's height until it drops.
- `autoShield` decays for every player; two players react to loose balls.
- Hooks: `__UF.testPass(d, pw)`, `__UF.testDribble(secs, turnDeg, sprint)`, `__UF.dribDbg(n)`; `__UF.testMove` fixed (it double-integrated position).

**Tests and results:**
- `testPass`: 10 m 0.72 s (14.4–18.6 m/s by power), 16 m 1.02 s, 25 m 1.33 s; power spans ~45%; a 10 m pass now stops at ~25 m (was 30+).
- `testDribble`: jog 1.4 touches/s, ball 0.82 m ahead (max 1.0); sprint 1.6/s, 0.94 m (max 1.2); 45° change 0.12 s; 90° 0.07 s; 180° 0.07 s; never lost at jog, lost once in a sprinting 180° (realistic).
- `testMove`: 90° cut at 7 m/s settles in 0.47 s keeping ≥2.7 m/s (was 1.7); 180° 0.25 s; 45° at 4 m/s 0.25 s.
- `simdiff --n=6`: see the table in the commit message; CPU wins rise monotonically with difficulty.
- 3D and 2D captures: no page errors; foot slide unchanged.

**Known limitations:** pass completion for the human side's AI team-mates is lower at high difficulty than before (faster arrivals are harder to control; by design the human side gets weaker mates). Heading is Phase 3.
**Files:** `src/game.js`, `docs/MIXAMO_LIST.md` (owner task), this file.

## Phase 3 — First touches, air balls, heading (2026-10-13)

## Phase 5 — Movement and animation, part A (2026-10-13)
**Implemented:** kicking-foot IK — every kick and dribble touch records the strike point relative to the body (`p.kbf/kbl/kbz`, snapshot d[16..18]); at the clip's contact moment (measured by tracing the foot through the shot and pass clips: lowest and fastest 0.075 s after the clip starts, 0.3 m ahead and 0.34 m to the kicking side) the renderer pulls the striking foot to that point with the two-bone solver (`kickIK`), weight peaking at contact; the simulation now launches kicks from ahead of the kicking foot (0.32 m forward, 0.34 m to the side) instead of straight ahead. Left-foot dribble touches (`touch_L` mirror, chosen by the foot that touched, d[15]). A pointing gesture when the pro calls for the ball (additive sub-clip of the point celebration, flag 8 in d[10]). Gait-variant plumbing (`VARIANTS` in clips.json → a third of players run a variant loop) is in but dormant: the extra CMU runs (09_xx/07_xx) are not in the mirror we fetch from. Stats: `__UF.footBall()` (ball-to-foot gap before/after IK for kicks and touches), `__UF.animSnap()` (bone angular change per frame, with the worst bone:clip), `__UF.stepDraw(n)` (simulation + render stepped together with every player AI-driven — needed because the headless clock crawls). Turn clips blend in over 0.16 s (was 0.08; their first pose caused ~90°/frame thigh snaps).
**Results (stepDraw 540 frames):** kicks: gap 15 cm before IK → **1.8 cm after** (target ≤5 cm); touches 15 → 2 cm when within reach (most dribble touches are beyond the additive touch clip's reach and keep their animation); bone snap avg 1.7°/frame, worst 89° at the first frame of a turn clip (now blended longer); foot slide unchanged.

## Fix round — random skills and stiff arms (2026-10-13, owner report)
**Inspected:** (1) AI skills: the carrier rolled a per-decision dice (`drib/2600 × vision`, ~0.03–0.07) every ~0.2 s whenever an opponent was within 2.6 m, with no cooldown, no "opponent ahead" test and no dribbling floor → several moves per minute from anyone, often with the defender behind them. Human Simple scheme: stick reversals, sideways flicks and sprint cuts fired skills with a 0.6 s cooldown and no opponent test, so ordinary direction changes read as random moves. (2) Sync: the sideways burst was applied the instant the move started while the clip's push-off comes 0.2–0.4 s in, so the body slid before the feet moved. (3) Arms: the CMU locomotion clips barely pump the arms on this rig; measured in a match the upper arm swung −53…+5° with the elbow almost locked at the back of the swing (14°), hands drifting straight down beside the shorts.
**Implemented:** AI moves only from dribblers (drib ≥ 60) with the opponent 1–3.2 m **ahead**, 6 s cooldown, a per-decision rate that gives about one move per 8–15 s of sustained pressure (more on harder levels), never inside their own defensive 8 m. Stick gestures now need an opponent ahead, a clear reversal (dot < −0.75 at > 3.5 m/s) or a hard cut above 6 m/s, a 1.5 s cooldown, and a Settings switch "Stick-gesture skills" (on by default; off = skill button only). Skill sync: the change of direction is a pending burst (`skillBurst`, `SK_T` per move: step-over 0.42 s, drag-back 0.22, ball roll 0.20, heel chop 0.24, roulette 0.30) applied at the clip's push-off frame while the carrier holds ~45% speed through the wind-up; the signature-skill branch uses the same path. Arms (`tools/blender/retarget.py`, character rebuilt): shoulders rotated 20° forward (was 7°), an elbow flexion floor (`ELBOW_SOFT`: +60° × e^(−angle/40°) about each elbow's learned hinge axis, from a prepass over the clip's bent frames), and an arm-swing gain about each locomotion clip's mean upper-arm pose (walk ×1.3, jog ×1.6, run ×1.8, sprint ×1.4) applied to arm, forearm and hand.
**Tests:** `__UF.testAISkill(600)` → 14 moves in 600 decisions under constant pressure (≈7 per minute of being closed down, i.e. a move every 8–9 s when a defender is right there; nothing without one); `__UF.testSkill('stepover')` still succeeds with the wrong-foot effect; `__UF.armStat(120)` on a 7.6 m/s runner → shoulder swing −52…+60° (was −53…+5°), elbow bend up to 103°, hand travel 0.83 m fore-aft (was 0.99 with no swing); viewer captures `captures/arms5/*` (run at 6 m/s: pumping arms, bent elbows); `scripts/acceptance.mjs` 29/29.
